About the School

Developing Economists for a Changing World

The School of Economics provides an environment for learning, research and professional development in economics and related fields.

Our academic structure will bring together departments and programmes designed to prepare students for opportunities in the public, private, academic and development sectors.

Quality Education Strong academic foundations and professional development.
Research Encouraging analytical thinking and economic research.
Innovation Preparing students for changing economic environments.
Professional Growth Building skills for meaningful careers.

Our Academic Community

The School of Economics brings together students, lecturers, researchers and professionals with a shared interest in understanding economies and improving economic outcomes.
